The reused binary already includes our drag turning, touch input bridge,
no pointer lock, no fullscreen/dialog helpers, single thread local play,
state observation, resize, pause and new-game patches. The original
SOURCE.txt and browser.patch in original-engine-source.tgz list every
inherited engine/build patch, and include corresponding source and SDL dependencies.

Web-only patches for this page, in source.tgz/patches/shareware-frame.patch:
- web/game.js: load unchanged DOOM1.WAD, isolate sensitivity storage,
  expose menu-aware keys, space and punctuation through a paginated touch drawer,
  add reverse weapon cycling/sensitivity and release every held text key,
  keep the touch drawer outside drag handling, supply integer 4:3 video dimensions
  before native video creation, and bound/cancel the readiness resize wait.
- web/index.html: Doom shareware title, persistent legal link and KEYS drawer.
- web/style.css: persistent legal link and compact responsive touch drawer.
Other supplied sources are exact files in source.tgz, separate from that patch:
- legal/: preserve publisher files and engine notices; replace Freedoom data credits
  with Doom shareware provenance and engine credits.
- proof/: remove machine/spawn assumptions, require own origin requests and
  zero dialogs, prove native touch menu actions and retain negative mutations;
  sample first native readiness and backing size atomically at DPR 3 with trusted
  touch, and exercise false-readiness timeout, cancellation and recovery.
No engine or game-data bytes were changed. Runtime JS and WASM retain their
original filenames freedoom.js and freedoom.wasm to make reuse explicit.

Corresponding source is provided by both archives in this directory:
original-engine-source.tgz is the unchanged source archive from the pinned page;
source.tgz provides this page's wrapper, patches, proof, notices and hosting docs.
The delivered repo.bundle provides branch main, including engine sources.
The upstream address is provenance text, not an external runtime request or link.
