OpenArena licences and source

This page uses the OpenArena 0.8.8 game and free data with the ioquake3 engine. Their original credits and licence notices are preserved. OpenArena is a community project; this browser adaptation is a separate distribution.

You may share and modify the GPL-covered program and data under the terms of GNU GPL version 2. GPL section 1 permits copying and distributing verbatim source in any medium. GPL section 3 permits object-code distribution with its corresponding source. These works are supplied without warranty. The selected maps are oa_dm1, aggressor and oa_minia.
