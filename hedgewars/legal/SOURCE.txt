Patches and additions, all GPL-2.0:
1. host/build.py adapts freshly compiled generated runtime, removes monolithic loader
   and old UI, rejects pointer lock/fullscreen imports, removes native prompt,
   omits credentials, caps DPR and uses ArrayBuffer WASM instantiation.
2. host/bridge.js reuses local IPC setup with two hotseat teams and selected maps.
   Removes network matchmaking/session storage and loads only the explicit data.
3. host/frame.js, index.html and style.css implement a responsive canvas, START,
   relative legal navigation, direct SDL touch inputs and supported local controls.
   Fix round 2 removes unsupported Capture/Record, suppresses C/R and enlarges
   existing touch targets. Engine, compiled runtime and game data are unchanged.
4. host/PlayObserver.lua reads real engine state for proof; no gameplay mutation.
5. host/rgba.mjs converts three paletted PNGs to RGBA with identical pixel values.
6. Nature/theme.cfg removes the music line. Unverified recordings are excluded; their virtual paths alias existing free effects.
7. The old required wqy-zenhei.ttc path aliases unchanged DejaVuSans-Bold.ttf bytes.
8. host/legal.py generates original notices, per-file ledger and source packages.
9. proof/run.sh and proof/check.mjs run the required static-frame and mutation proof.
10. host/deliver.py archives the exact-head passing site, source bundle and proof,
    splits transport parts at 24 MB, and writes sizes and checksum receipts.

No engine C/Pascal/Rust or physics patch. host/compile.py adds a missing Lua header
through a compiler include flag and compiles the upstream generated C directly. No service worker, threads, payments, account,
analytics, external requests, browser persistence or multiplayer transport.
